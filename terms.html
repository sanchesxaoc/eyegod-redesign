Privacy & data security

Privacy is a structural property of the platform, not a policy bolted on afterwards. Every search and every result is protected by end-to-end asymmetric encryption.

4.1End-to-end encryption

Searches and results are encrypted end-to-end using asymmetric encryption. Nobody on our side can read the content of your queries or your results. Encryption is applied both at rest and in transit.

4.2Retention and automatic deletion

All search data and results are subject to a strict 30-day retention window. After that period the data is automatically and permanently deleted, with no exceptions and no possibility of recovery. We do not retain queries, results or investigation logs beyond that window under any circumstances.

Deletion is irreversibleBecause deletion is permanent and unrecoverable, export anything you need for your records before the 30-day window closes. We cannot restore deleted search history, even at your request.

4.3No third-party sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your personal data or your search activity to any third party for their own purposes. How we handle the account data we do collect is described in full in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these Terms.

Subscription & payment

5.1Subscription plans

We offer monthly and yearly subscription plans. Subscriptions are prepaid and activate immediately upon payment confirmation. Each plan includes a defined monthly credit allowance and an API rate limit, both published on the pricing page.

5.2Credits

What a credit is
One credit equals one successful lookup that returns data.
Free outcomes
Empty result sets on API v2, cached results, discovery calls and webhook test pings do not consume credits.
Expiry
Monthly allowances reset at the start of each billing period and do not roll over.
Exhaustion
When your balance reaches zero, lookups return HTTP 402 until the next reset or an upgrade.

5.3Payment processing

Payments are handled by authorized third-party processors. We never store full payment card numbers on our servers. Available payment methods are shown at checkout.

5.4Refund policy

All sales are final. Refunds may be considered case by case, exclusively for technical faults that prevent access to the Service. Contact support@eyegod.cc with your purchase confirmation to open a request.

What is not refundableDissatisfaction with search results, absence of data for a given target, changes to third-party module availability, and accounts terminated for policy violations are not grounds for a refund.

5.5Renewal and cancellation

Recurring subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled beforehand from the dashboard. Cancelling stops future charges; it does not refund the period already paid, and access continues until that period ends.
