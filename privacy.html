Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it — written to be read, not skimmed past.

Effective January 1, 2026 Terms of Service Privacy Policy FAQ

In short

  • We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to brokers, not to anyone.
  • Queries are encrypted. We cannot read the content of your searches.
  • 30-day deletion. Search data is destroyed automatically and permanently.

Introduction

At eyegod.cc we are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have over it.

This policy covers the eyegod.cc website, the dashboard and the public API. It does not cover the content of the third-party datasets accessible through the Service, which originate outside our control. By using the platform you agree to the practices described here.

01

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, information gathered automatically as you use the Service, and — in limited cases — information from third-party providers such as our payment processors.

1.1Information you provide

Account data
Email address, username and a hashed password when you register.
Payment data
Billing details processed by our payment providers. Full card numbers are never stored on our servers.
Communications
Messages, feedback and support requests you send us, including any attachments.
API credentials
API keys generated for your account, stored in encrypted form.

1.2Information collected automatically

Usage data
Pages visited, features used and interaction patterns within the dashboard.
Device data
IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers and time zone.
Log data
Server logs including access timestamps, request paths, status codes and error reports.
Cookies
Session and persistent cookies used to maintain authentication and remember preferences.

1.3A note on your search queries

Search queries occupy a special category. They are encrypted end-to-end using asymmetric encryption, which means we cannot read their content. What we can see is operational metadata — that a query occurred, its type, its timestamp and whether it consumed a credit — which is what makes billing and abuse prevention possible.

Metadata, not contentWe know that you ran an email lookup at 14:32 and that it cost one credit. We do not know which email address you looked up. All of it is deleted after 30 days regardless.

1.4Information we do not collect

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government identity documents, biometric data, precise geolocation or health information about our users. We do not purchase user profiles from data brokers, and we do not build advertising profiles.

03

Data sharing & disclosure

We share information only in the limited circumstances described below.

3.1Service providers

We work with vendors that perform services on our behalf — payment processing, email delivery, cloud hosting and analytics. They are contractually bound to handle your data securely, to use it only for the purposes we specify, and to delete it when the relationship ends.

3.2Legal requirements

We may disclose information where required by law, or where we hold a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, to protect our rights or property, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to protect the personal safety of users or the public.

What we can actually produceBecause search queries are end-to-end encrypted and deleted after 30 days, there is a hard limit on what any legal request can recover. We cannot produce the content of queries we are technically unable to read, nor data that has already been destroyed.

3.3Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you by email or through a prominent notice on the platform before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

3.4Aggregate and anonymized data

We may publish aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you — for example, overall platform usage statistics or breach coverage metrics.

06

Data security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, destruction and alteration.

Transport encryption
TLS 1.3 for all traffic between your browser or client and our servers.
Encryption at rest
Sensitive fields are encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in recoverable form.
End-to-end query encryption
Asymmetric encryption applied to searches and results, so query content is unreadable to us.
Access control
Role-based controls restrict which internal personnel can reach any user data, on a least-privilege basis.
Auditing
Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments across our infrastructure.
Anomaly detection
Automated monitoring for unusual login patterns, credential sharing and API abuse.
No system is perfectWe take every reasonable precaution, but no security system is impenetrable. In the event of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law and publish details of the impact and our response.

6.1What you can do

  • Use a unique, high-entropy password that you do not reuse anywhere else.
  • Store API keys in a secret manager or environment variable — never in source control.
  • Issue a separate API key per service so a single leak has a limited blast radius.
  • Revoke keys you no longer use, and rotate the rest on a schedule.
  • Report anything suspicious to support@eyegod.cc immediately.
07

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information. We honour these requests regardless of your location, subject to legal retention requirements.

RightWhat it means
AccessRequest a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
CorrectionAsk us to fix inaccurate or incomplete information.
DeletionRequest erasure of your personal data, subject to legal retention rules.
PortabilityReceive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
ObjectionObject to processing based on our legitimate interests.
RestrictionAsk us to limit how we process your data in specific circumstances.
Withdraw consentWithdraw consent at any time where processing relies on it.

7.1How to exercise your rights

Email support@eyegod.cc from the address registered to your account, describing which right you wish to exercise. We respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, which protects you against someone impersonating you to obtain your data.

7.2Opting out of marketing

You can opt out of marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message we send, or by contacting us directly. You cannot opt out of transactional email — password resets, security alerts and billing notices — because they are necessary to operate your account.

7.3Lodging a complaint

If you believe we have mishandled your personal data, we would like the chance to put it right — contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

08

International transfers

We operate globally, which means your information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in. Those countries may have different data protection laws from your own.

Wherever data is processed, we apply the same technical and organizational safeguards described in this policy, and we use appropriate transfer mechanisms — such as standard contractual clauses — where the law requires them.

09

Third-party services

The platform may contain links to third-party websites, integrations or services. This Privacy Policy applies only to eyegod.cc. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their policies before interacting with them.

The third-party services we rely on include payment processors, cloud infrastructure providers, email delivery services and upstream intelligence providers. Each operates under its own privacy policy and under data processing agreements with us.

10

eyegod.cc is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from someone under 18, contact us immediately at support@eyegod.cc and we will delete it promptly.
