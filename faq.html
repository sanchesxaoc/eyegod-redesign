Plans & billing

6 questions

Four paid tiers: Basic (180 credits/mo), Starter (800), Professional (2,000) and Enterprise (unlimited, plus premium third-party sources). Monthly and yearly billing are both available — see the pricing section.

One credit is one successful lookup that returns data. On API v2, queries returning zero results are not charged, and neither are cached results, discovery calls or webhook test pings. Full breakdown in the billing documentation.

All sales are final. Refunds are considered case by case, exclusively for technical issues that prevent access to the platform. Contact support@eyegod.cc with your purchase confirmation to open a request.

Yes — API access is included on every paid plan, with rate limits scaling by tier (60 to unlimited requests per minute). Full documentation, code samples and webhook guides live at /docs.

Payments run through our authorized processors; available methods are shown at checkout. We never store card details on our servers.

Lookups return HTTP 402 until your allowance resets at the start of the next billing period, or until you upgrade. Nothing is deleted and your account stays active — only new queries are paused.
